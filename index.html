Serasé Today · DCB Only

DCB remains useful and is not proposed for removal. The gap is that Cards, FPX / Online Banking and E-wallets are not currently available as parallel checkout routes.

Current: DCB

Business Risk if We Stay DCB-only

Payment-method mismatch · Checkout friction · Carrier dependency · Reduced payment flexibility. These are business risks, not measured Serasé conversion-loss percentages.

CardsFPX / BankingE-wallets
Recommended Direction · Add, Don't Replace
Keep DCB + add Open Payment Gateway options. Malaysia is already a multi-payment market, so broader checkout coverage gives digitally acquired users more ways to complete payment while retaining the carrier-billing route.
PayNet · 8.44B Transactions ↗ BNM Annual Report ↗
DCB + MORE Cards · FPX / Banking · E-wallets Gateway pricing / MDR / setup fees: TBC
Management takeaway: We are paying to acquire users through open digital channels, but today every paying user must exit through a single DCB payment route. Adding payment methods broadens checkout access; it does not mean removing DCB.
