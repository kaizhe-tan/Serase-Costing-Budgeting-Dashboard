09 Costing Analysis Three-year affordability, unit economics and funding implications
Executive Financial Assessment · 3-Year View

Growth is achievable, but the current cost structure is not yet self-funding.

The model projects meaningful subscriber growth and RM 4.10 million in net subscription revenue. However, eKYC, infrastructure and acquisition costs create a material funding gap, while Year 3 unit economics remain below the 3.0x LTV:CAC target.

Board-level verdict Proceed as a funded growth investment Do not treat Year 1 as a break-even launch. Lock down AI costs, improve acquisition efficiency and raise monetisation or retention before scale.

Core assumptions

Model basis

  • 256,268 paid verifications over three years; monthly volume rises from 1,739 to 14,502.
  • MAU grows from approximately 1k to 179k; AWS costs above 50k MAU are extrapolated.
  • FX rate is USD 1 = RM 4.09 and paid acquisition CPA is USD 3.
  • Quota overage is extended at the quoted plan rate for like-for-like comparison.
  • Salaries and Innov8tif optional modules are excluded.
